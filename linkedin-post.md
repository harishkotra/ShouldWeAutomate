I built an open-source tool that tells you if your business workflow is ready for AI automation — and no, it doesn't just say "yes" or "no."

It's called ShouldWeAutomate? and it evaluates workflows across 7 dimensions:

📊 Data Quality — is your data clean enough?
🔧 Process Stability — does the process change every month?
⚠️ Exception Rate — how often do humans need to step in?
🧠 Decision Complexity — are the rules clear or subjective?
🔗 Integration Readiness — do your systems have APIs?
⚖️ Governance Risk — what happens when it goes wrong?
💰 ROI Potential — is it actually worth automating?

Each dimension gets scored, weighted, and combined into one overall readiness score with a clear recommendation tier — from "DO NOT AUTOMATE" to "AGENT AUTOMATION READY."

The best part? You don't need to manually fiddle with 35 sliders. Write a paragraph describing your workflow, and the optional AI engine pre-fills everything. Then tweak, simulate what-if scenarios, and even get remediation playbooks.

Built with Flask (Python), vanilla JavaScript, and Chart.js. Fully open-source — contributions welcome.
