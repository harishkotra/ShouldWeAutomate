About This Platform

Built by skeptics, for pragmatists.

The Framework

Seven weighted dimensions produce a single readiness score from 0-100. Recommendations range from DO NOT AUTOMATE to AGENT AUTOMATION READY.

Scoring Philosophy

Higher governance risk = lower score. Poor data quality caps readiness regardless of other dimensions. We're biased toward human-in-the-loop systems when uncertainty exists.

Synthetic Benchmark

600+ generated workflows across 10 industries with deliberately injected failure modes: contradictory rules, broken APIs, regulatory churn, missing data, seasonal spikes, fraud scenarios, multilingual documents, legacy ERP integrations.
